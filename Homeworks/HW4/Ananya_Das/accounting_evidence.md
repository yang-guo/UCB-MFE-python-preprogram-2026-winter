# Part 2: performance metrics

Formulas: CAGR = (V_T/V_0)^(1/years) − 1, where years = calendar days / 365.25. Daily return r_t = V_t/V_{t−1} − 1. Daily risk-free rate = 1.02^(1/252) − 1. Sharpe = √252 × mean(r − rf_d) / sample std(r). Annualized volatility = sample std(r) × √252. Max drawdown = min(V_t / running peak − 1). The benchmark is $1,000,000 × P_t / P_first, bought at the first trading close.

# Part 3: purchase schedule and eligibility

Ramp-up: on the first session of each of the first 3 months, spend min(cash, NAV/3). After that: on the session after a sale, spend min(cash, sale proceeds at the previous close). A stock is eligible if it is in the universe list for the entry's quarter (selected by the signal close) and has prices on ≥60 of the 63 sessions ending at the signal date.
